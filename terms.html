What clipfarm is

clipfarm is a private program run by the owner of the mAgNiFiCeNt_cLiPs channel. It cuts short videos from gameplay and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts. On TikTok it sends each video to the account's inbox as a draft; the owner opens the draft in the TikTok app and posts it by hand.

Who can use it

Only the channel's owner. clipfarm is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and cannot be used with anyone else's TikTok account.

What gets uploaded

  • The owner's own gameplay recordings.
  • Clips of streamers who gave permission to be clipped, or whose clipping program invites clips. Each one shows the streamer's name on screen and credits their channel in the post.
  • Every video is looked at and approved by the owner before it is uploaded.

Taking a clip down

If you are a streamer or a rights holder and want a clip removed, message @magnificent_clips0 on TikTok or @magnificent_clips on YouTube. It is removed the same day we read the message.

TikTok's own rules

Use of TikTok through clipfarm follows TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Developer Terms. If these terms and TikTok's ever disagree, TikTok's apply.

No warranty

clipfarm is provided as it is, for the owner's own use, with no warranty of any kind.

For streamers

How we ask, how we credit you, and how to get a clip removed.

We ask first

If you got a message from mAgNiFiCeNt_cLiPs, it was a request to clip your GTA streams. Say yes, no, or name your conditions. A no is final: you go on a do-not-contact list and get no more messages.

What a yes covers

  • Short clips of your streams on the site where you gave the yes. A yes on Twitch is for your Twitch streams, a yes on Kick for your Kick streams.
  • Your name on screen in every clip and a link to your channel in every post.
  • You can take the yes back at any time.

Clipping programs

If you run a paid clipping program, we follow its rules: the footage it names, its hashtags and its required text.

Taking a clip down

Message @magnificent_clips0 on TikTok or @magnificent_clips on YouTube and say which clip. It is removed the same day we read the message. You do not need to give a reason.

What we keep about you

Your public channel name, and your reply to our message as the record of your answer. Ask the same way and we delete it. Details are in the privacy policy.
